## Interpreting the Order

`CollectVote(from N)` records the source ID when the receiving replica handles that vote. In the voting machine, that log happens before stale-block rejection and certificate verification; verification also runs asynchronously. So this is the handler’s observed arrival order, **not necessarily the accepted-vote order or the QC signer order**. The views show a stable collector rotation, but vote arrival order is not deterministic across runs.
